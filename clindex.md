# CLIndex
[CLindex](https://singleron.bio/products/clindex/) allows multiplexing single cell libraries through click-chemistry-based sample tagging.

## Cell line mixture 1

1:1:1:1 mixture of NB4, THP-1, U937 and CCRF

- HTML report(4.40M): https://singleronbio-opendata.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/datasets/clindex/tag_report.html

- Matrix(30.16M): https://singleronbio-opendata.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/datasets/clindex/output.tar

- fastq(510.23M): https://singleronbio-opendata.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/datasets/clindex/fastq.tar
