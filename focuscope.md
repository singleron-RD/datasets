# FocusScope

The [FocuSCOPE](https://singleron.bio/products/focuscope-single-cell-targeted-capture-kit/) single Cell Targeted Capture Kit utilizes targeted customized probes for simultaneous whole transcriptome sequencing.

## Blood snp 1

- fastq(5.75G): https://singleronbio-opendata.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/datasets/focuscope/blood_snp_1/fastq.tar
